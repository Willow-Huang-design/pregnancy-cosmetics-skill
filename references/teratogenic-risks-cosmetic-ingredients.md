# Source reference: Teratogenic Risks of Cosmetic Ingredients During Pregnancy

Source distilled from the user-provided PDF `TERATOGENIC_RISKS_OF_COSMETIC_INGREDIENTS_DURING_P.pdf` and the workspace file `孕期化妆品成分致畸风险_蒸馏知识库.md`.

## Scope

Qualitative narrative review of three ingredient groups: inorganic mercury in whitening/bleaching products, hydroquinone (benzene-1,4-diol/quinol), and retinoic acid/retinoids in acne or dermatologic products. The review concludes that pregnant users should avoid these three groups, but it does not conclude that all makeup or skincare is forbidden.

## Use in answers

- Attribute the avoid-oriented conclusion to this review, not to a universal guideline.
- Preserve the review type and its limitations: qualitative literature synthesis, no unified individual dose-response estimate, and no complete product-category assessment.
- Do not add salicylic acid, parabens, phthalates, benzophenones, or metals to this source unless another source explicitly supports them.
