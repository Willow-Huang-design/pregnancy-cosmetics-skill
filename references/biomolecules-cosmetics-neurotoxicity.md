Source distilled from the user-provided PDF `biomolecules-14-00984.pdf` and the workspace file `孕期化妆品神经毒性_蒸馏知识库.md`.

## Scope

Narrative review: *Use of Cosmetics in Pregnancy and Neurotoxicity: Can It Increase the Risk of Congenital Enteric Neuropathies?* It discusses microplastics/nanoplastics, parabens, benzophenones (including BP-3/oxybenzone), phthalates (DEP/DBP/DMP), and cosmetic metals. It describes dermal, inhalation, and oral/near-mouth exposure; reports detection in maternal blood/urine, placenta, umbilical cord, and breast milk; and discusses possible neurotoxicity/ENS-development mechanisms.

## Evidence guardrails

- The review emphasizes early pregnancy as a sensitive developmental window and says some substances can cross the placental barrier or occur in breast milk.
- It explicitly acknowledges that dangerous concentrations, realistic human exposure models, and direct causal relationships remain unclear.
- It does not provide trimester-specific bans, safe thresholds, brand advice, or universal substitutes.
- It does not specifically review retinoic acid/retinol, salicylic acid, or hydroquinone.
